01 / My Story

Curiosity is my superpower.

I've spent more than two decades exploring how people, businesses, technology and ideas work together — and what happens when they don't.

My career hasn't followed a traditional path, and I'm grateful for that. From military service, healthcare and entrepreneurship to marketing strategy and enterprise software engineering, every experience has shaped how I see the world.

I've worked with government agencies, global technology companies, large enterprises, universities and small businesses. Regardless of the industry, I find myself asking the same questions:

Why are we doing it this way? Who are we solving this for? What are we missing? And how can we make it better?

I'm equally comfortable discussing brand strategy, analyzing business processes, designing digital experiences, architecting enterprise solutions or sitting down and writing code.

I'm passionate about emerging technology, but I don't believe technology is the answer to everything. I believe in human creativity, collaboration, critical thinking and the extraordinary things people can accomplish when given the right tools and opportunities.

I'm not afraid to challenge assumptions, admit what I don't know or ask the seemingly obvious question.

I want to leave things better than I found them. And if I can change a little piece of the world along the way, even better.

06 / Perspectives

A few things I believe.

Technology should empower people, not diminish them.

Innovation doesn't always require invention. Sometimes it requires simplification.

Creativity is not a problem artificial intelligence needs to solve.

The best ideas don't always come from the person with the highest title.

A good question can be more valuable than a thousand lines of code.

Never confuse complexity with intelligence.

Success should be measured by what we make better, not just what we make.

And yes, I still believe we can change the world.
